# Typeset copies

These are copies of the 19 subject files with every typographic rule applied,
including the two that were left alone in the originals. Nothing here is a
content change: no pearl was added, removed or reworded, and no file's word
count moved by more than 1%.

## What differs from the originals

| Rule                     | Originals                                      | Here                                           |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Bold                     | 50.7% of body text                             | 2.9% — only the `PEARL:` / `MUST KNOW:` labels |
| All caps                 | 9.9% of body text, 431 runs longer than a line | 1.4%, no run longer than a line                |
| Bold and italic together | used                                           | never; `***x***` became `*x*`                  |
| Ampersands               | in headings                                    | "and", with the Contents anchors rebuilt       |

Curly quotes, downward apostrophes, real ellipses, single spaces between
sentences and correct dashes were already applied to the originals, so they
carry over unchanged.

## How capitals were decided

A capitalised word is folded to lower case only when the same word is used in
lower case somewhere in the 19 files, so `DRY` became `dry` while `ICP`, `ECF`,
`FIGO` and `SIADH` kept their capitals. Words that appear elsewhere capitalised
as names were restored as names — `ARLT'S` to `Arlt's`, `LEBER` to `Leber`.

Two residues are deliberate:

- Rare terms that only ever appear shouted in these notes — `MIRAGE`,
  `CLADOSPORIUM`, `ABIRATERONE` — have no lower-case evidence in the corpus, so
  they were left alone rather than guessed at. A single word in capitals is
  permitted by the rule in any case.
- Section labels stay in capitals because each is far shorter than one line,
  and they carry the structure of every pearl.

## Reading these

The CSS snippet at `.obsidian/snippets/typography.css` applies to this folder
too. With almost no bold left, you may want to raise `--line-height-normal`
back towards 1.35 here — light text needs more leading than heavy text, which
is the opposite of what the originals needed.
